One line roasting the smart fridge I share a house with: it locked the water dispenser until I renewed its filter subscription, so now I pay monthly rent to a box that keeps my own ice hostage.

> My smart fridge locked the water dispenser until I renewed its filter subscription, so now I pay monthly rent to a box that keeps my own ice hostage.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the roast to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 160 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line, and not only whitespace.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
